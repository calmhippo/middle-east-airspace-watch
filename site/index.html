Reading the map

Each line is one civil flight that crossed the region (Egypt to Pakistan, Yemen to the Caucasus) in the week shown, from takeoff to landing. Thicker, darker lines carry more flights.

Gulf–Europe routes

Flights between Gulf airports and Europe, by where they cross latitude 34°N: west of Iraq (over Saudi Arabia, Jordan, Syria or Egypt), over Iraq, or over Iran. With this tab open, the map marks that line, split at the Syria–Iraq and Iraq–Iran borders (40.1°E and 45.4°E).

Gaps in coverage

Positions come from volunteer receivers. Few cover Saudi Arabia's interior, Iraq, Iran and the open sea, so there a flight is drawn as a straight line between the last and next report. An empty area can mean airlines avoid it or that no receiver hears it, so compare the same area across weeks.
